Our Core Privacy Promise

THUNA is built from the ground up to be private by default. We do not require accounts, we do not inspect or sell your file content, and all uploaded and processed files are automatically purged from our servers within 1 hour.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. When you use THUNA, we only collect the minimum information strictly necessary to provide our conversion and compression services:

  • Uploaded Files: The files you upload (PDFs, Word documents, images, audio, video, spreadsheets) are held temporarily in an isolated server directory solely for the duration needed to perform your requested operation.
  • Anonymous Session Tokens: When you access THUNA, an ephemeral session token is generated in your browser. This token links your browser to your conversion results so only you can download them. It contains no personal identifiable information.
  • Technical Logs: Standard web server logs (IP address, user-agent, timestamp, HTTP response status) may be briefly stored for infrastructure monitoring, DDoS defense, and rate limiting. These logs are automatically rotated and deleted.

2. How We Process and Store Your Files

Your files undergo safe, automated processing:

  • Server-Side Only: All processing operations (converting, compressing, merging) occur in isolated memory or temporary sandboxed directories on our secure servers.
  • Zero Manual Inspection: No human ever views, previews, or reads your files. Processing is 100% automated by our conversion engines.
  • Automatic Deletion: Uploaded input files and generated output files are permanently deleted after 1 hour or when your browser session ends. We do not maintain backups or archives of user files.
  • No Model Training: Your files and documents are never used to train machine learning models, AI systems, or shared with data brokers.

3. Cookies and Browser Storage

THUNA uses only strictly necessary cookies and browser storage:

  • CSRF Token Cookie: A cryptographic token (`csrftoken`) required to protect your requests against Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks.
  • Session Cookie: An anonymous session identifier (`sessionid`) used to deliver your converted files back to your device securely.
  • Local Storage: We store your cookie consent preference (`thuna_cookie_consent`) locally on your device so the consent prompt does not reappear.
  • No Advertising Cookies: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, marketing pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts.

4. Third-Party Services and Sub-processors

THUNA operates on self-contained, native server engines. We do not stream your file contents to external proprietary SaaS APIs. If external binaries (such as open-source FFmpeg, LibreOffice, or Tesseract OCR) are utilized, they run locally within our sandboxed server perimeter.

5. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Regulations)

Under international privacy laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data is processed (as detailed here, strictly minimal ephemeral processing).
  • Request immediate deletion of your files (which happens automatically within 1 hour).
  • Object to data selling or sharing (we never sell, license, or share user data).

Because we do not require accounts, registration, or emails, we do not maintain persistent user profiles or databases of identities.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our security practices, you can contact our privacy team at thuna@gmail.com.
